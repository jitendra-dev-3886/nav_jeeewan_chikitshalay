1. Executive Summary
This SRS defines a professional, mobile-first digital platform for Nav Jeevan Chikitsalaya. The solution is designed for local patient discovery, trust building, appointment requests and efficient day-to-day appointment administration. The initial release avoids unnecessary hospital-management complexity and keeps the experience practical for a single clinic or small team.
1.1 Product Vision
Create a fast, trustworthy and accessible clinic website that helps patients understand services, contact the clinic and book a visit in under two minutes, while allowing clinic staff to manage availability and appointments from a simple admin dashboard.
1.2 Business Goals
Increase appointment enquiries from Google Search, Maps, social media and WhatsApp.
Reduce phone-call dependency and manual diary errors.
Present verified doctor, clinic, service and timing information consistently.
Give the clinic an owned digital presence that can grow into a patient portal later.
Provide measurable insights: enquiries, bookings, sources, cancellations and popular services.

5. Core User Journeys
5.1 Patient Books an Appointment
Patient opens the website from search, map, QR code or social link.
Patient reviews the doctor/service and selects Book Appointment.
System displays valid dates and available slots in clinic local time.
Patient enters minimum details, accepts consent text and verifies mobile if enabled.
System creates a pending or confirmed appointment based on clinic policy.
Patient receives a reference and communication; staff sees the booking immediately.
Patient may reschedule or cancel through a time-limited secure link.
5.2 Reception Confirms and Completes a Visit
Receptionist signs in and opens today’s queue.
Pending request is reviewed for slot conflicts and confirmed or rescheduled.
On arrival, status is changed to Checked in.
After consultation, status is changed to Completed.
System logs each change and updates dashboard metrics.

7. UI/UX Specification
7.1 Design Direction
The experience should feel calm, credible and contemporary—not like a crowded hospital portal. Use the existing dark green and navy identity with generous white space, restrained shadows, rounded cards and clear typography. Medical red is reserved for urgent warnings, not normal calls to action.

7.2 Homepage Structure
Utility strip: phone, hours and emergency disclaimer.
Header: logo, concise navigation, language option and Book Appointment button.
Hero: clear clinic benefit, doctor identity, trust facts and two primary actions.
Service cards: six or fewer primary services with View Details.
Why choose us: verified facts only—no unsubstantiated “best clinic” claims.
Doctor section, clinic timings, appointment CTA, testimonials, map and footer.
7.3 Booking UX Rules
Use a short multi-step form with progress indication and clear back navigation.
Do not request diagnosis, ID proof or unnecessary sensitive data during initial booking.
Show validation beside the relevant field and preserve entered data after errors.
Use plain Hindi/English labels; avoid medical jargon.
Display confirmation, clinic address, timing, cancellation method and “not for emergencies” notice.
Sticky Book / Call / WhatsApp actions on small screens without covering content.
7.4 Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA principles: keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings, labels, alt text and sufficient contrast.
Support zoom and responsive reflow; avoid information communicated by colour alone.
Provide descriptive errors and status announcements for assistive technology.
Respect reduced-motion preference and keep animation optional and brief.

10. Security, Privacy and Safety
HTTPS everywhere; secure cookies; CSRF protection; strict CORS allow-list; security headers and content security policy.
Passwords hashed with a current adaptive algorithm; optional multi-factor authentication for administrators.
Role-based authorization enforced on the Laravel API, never only in the React UI.
Validate and sanitize all input; parameterized queries through ORM/query builder; safe upload type/size inspection.
Rate limits, bot protection and login throttling; lockout alerts without revealing account existence.
Encrypt sensitive backups and secrets; keep keys outside source control; rotate credentials when staff changes.
Minimize patient data, capture explicit consent, define retention/deletion rules and support correction requests.
Mask mobile numbers and personal data in logs, analytics, notifications and non-production environments.
Audit privileged actions and exports; restrict production database access by need.
Display a prominent instruction that the website is not an emergency service and does not provide automated medical advice.
Before launch, the clinic should obtain appropriate legal/privacy review for the jurisdictions and healthcare workflows it serves.

14. Phase 1 Acceptance Criteria
A patient can find verified clinic information and complete a valid booking on a mobile device.
Unavailable or closed slots cannot be booked, including during concurrent requests.
Reception staff can create, confirm, reschedule, cancel, check in and complete an appointment.
Clinic admin can manage services, availability, closures, essential content and users without code changes.
Configured confirmation/reminder messages are queued and their delivery status is visible.
Public forms have validation, consent, spam/rate controls and helpful error states.
Role permissions are enforced by API tests; privileged actions appear in the audit log.
Responsive, accessibility, browser, backup-restore and launch smoke tests pass.
All published medical, practitioner and contact claims are approved by the client.
